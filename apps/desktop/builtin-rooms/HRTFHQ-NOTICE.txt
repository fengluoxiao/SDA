SDA HRTFHQ room catalog, 2026-10-26

The active catalog uses native-prepared apple-hrtfhq-542-v1 receivers, including
the shared common-ear clarity preparation. Synthetic measurement provenance
and calibration are recorded in assets/hrtf/apple-hrtfhq-v1. No music or user
recordings are part of these room assets. This is shared SDA convolution of
public AVAudioEnvironmentNode HRTFHQ transfer measurements, not personalized
Spatial Audio or an Apple API running on Windows or Android.

pyroomacoustics 0.10.1 supplies the image-source simulation. Room dimensions,
material coverage and placement are design assumptions. The native room
compositor supplies the direct/early/full energy references. One receiver
filter is used per reflection path; the selected direct HRTF is retained once.
These are simulated reference rooms, not measured or certified studios.

The existing NOTICE.txt and LICENSE-SADIE.txt apply to legacy KU100 archives
retained for loading saved profile IDs. They do not describe the new active
HRTFHQ catalog. The KU100 generation path has been removed.
