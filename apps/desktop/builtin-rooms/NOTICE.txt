Modifications by SDA: original 48 kHz HRIR measurements used as receiver filters
in pyroomacoustics image-source room simulation; layouts, room dimensions,
absorption assumptions, propagation delays and reference level comparisons
generated by scripts/build-builtin-rooms.mjs and scripts/room-simulator.py.
These are simulated reference rooms, not measured Atmos studios, personalized
ear responses, Genelec speaker emulations, or Dolby-certified room designs.
The source is an ideal omnidirectional emitter with no additional speaker FIR.
The SADIE HRIR retains its original measurement-speaker response and latency.
No DIRPAT measurement data is included in these built-in derivatives.
